Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PTD Today and PTD Today Cosmos handle information when you use ptdtoday.com or the PTD Today Cosmos ChatGPT plugin.

Summary: Cosmos processes the information needed to answer your question and operate the experience. PTD Today does not sell your personal information and does not use Cosmos questions to build advertising profiles.

1. Information we process

Questions and tool inputs

When you ask Cosmos a question, PTD Today may receive the question and limited context needed to answer it. Depending on the experience, that context can include the current topic or station, journey context, page URL, and other information necessary to produce the requested Cosmos view.

Browser-local Cosmos state

Cosmos uses browser storage to maintain features such as topic history, journey state, view state, and an anonymous presence-session identifier. Session storage is generally removed when the browser session ends. Local storage remains on the device until it is cleared by the user or browser.

Live presence

Where the live-presence feature is available, Cosmos may send an anonymous random session identifier and a station key so it can display an aggregate “here now” count. The public interface is not designed to expose your name, account, profile, or IP address to other observers.

Shared stations

If you deliberately use a sharing feature, Cosmos may store the station information needed to make the shared link work. Shared content can include the station title, summary, sources, relationships, projection data, and related journey context included in the share request.

Technical and usage information

Hosting, security, and network providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, request time, browser or device information, request metadata, and error or security logs. Some PTD Today pages also use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and referrals.

2. How we use information

  • To answer questions and generate Cosmos stations, relationships, evidence views, and scenario paths.
  • To maintain journey, topic, presence, and sharing features.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve PTD Today and Cosmos.
  • To understand aggregate usage and referral patterns on pages where analytics is enabled.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate failures, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Service providers and disclosures

PTD Today relies on service providers to operate Cosmos. Information may be processed by:

  • Cloudflare, for hosting, routing, security, Worker execution, and related infrastructure.
  • OpenAI, when AI processing is used to generate or support Cosmos responses and when the PTD Today Cosmos plugin is invoked inside ChatGPT.
  • Google Analytics, on PTD Today pages where analytics is enabled, for aggregate traffic and referral measurement.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect the security or integrity of the service, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganization. PTD Today does not sell personal information.

4. Retention

  • Session-storage data: generally remains until the relevant browser session or tab data is cleared.
  • Local-storage data: remains on your device until you or your browser clears it.
  • Live-presence state: is intended to be temporary and used only to calculate current aggregate presence.
  • Shared-station data: may persist so that a share link continues to work, unless the content is removed, the feature is retired, or deletion is requested and can be verified.
  • Operational, analytics, and infrastructure records: are retained only as needed for service operation, security, troubleshooting, analytics, or legal obligations, subject to PTD Today settings and the retention practices of the relevant service provider.

5. Your choices and controls

  • You can clear your browser's local and session storage to remove locally stored Cosmos state.
  • You can use browser privacy controls to restrict cookies or analytics where supported.
  • You can choose not to use the share feature if you do not want a station made accessible through a share link.
  • You may request removal of PTD Today-controlled shared content or ask a privacy question through the Support page.

6. Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, government identification numbers, payment-card information, or other secrets to Cosmos. Avoid providing sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for the question you are asking.

7. Children

PTD Today Cosmos is not directed to children under 13, and PTD Today does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

8. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Those locations may have different data-protection laws.

9. Changes to this policy

PTD Today may update this Privacy Policy as Cosmos evolves. The “Last updated” date below identifies the current version. Material changes may be reflected on this page before or when they take effect.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the PTD Today Cosmos Support page.
