Hitachi's Materials Informatics- AI for Materials Development - Hitachi

Hitachi presents Materials Informatics, an AI-driven approach to accelerate and diversify materials development. The solution addresses limits of empirical methods and lengthy simulations/experiments by making more effective use of data to discover new materials.

Watch on YouTube
Key points
  • Introduces Hitachi's Materials Informatics (MI) solutions for AI-assisted materials development.
  • Frames conventional material design as slow and empirically driven, requiring extensive simulation and testing.
  • MI uses multifaceted AI approaches to leverage data for faster discovery of previously undiscovered materials.
  • Aims to shorten development timelines and expand novelty in material design.
  • Positions materials informatics as part of Hitachi's digital transformation efforts (AI + DX).
So what
  • Materials informatics can shorten R&D cycles and lower time-to-deployment for new materials that affect infrastructure components.
  • Faster discovery of novel materials may influence future specifications for insulation, conductors, thermal management, and component materials.
  • Utility, EPC, and data‑center power stakeholders should track MI advances as they can shift procurement timelines and reliability/maintenance planning.
  • Integrating AI-driven materials data into DX strategies could provide competitive advantage in asset resilience and performance.
