Understanding Interconnection Queues and Study Process in Power Systems

Based on the title metadata, this video provides an overview of interconnection queues and the study process used to assess new generation and load requests on power systems. It appears to cover how projects progress through queue stages, the studies performed to determine network impacts, and the interfaces between developers, transmission owners, and system operators.

Watch on YouTube
Key points
  • Purpose of interconnection queues: manage requests to connect generation and large loads to the grid.
  • Typical study sequence: initial feasibility screening, system-impact studies, and detailed facilities studies.
  • Key outputs: identify required network upgrades, cost allocation, and technical/operational constraints.
  • Stakeholder roles: developers, transmission owners, and regional/system operators coordinate through the process.
  • Common challenges: long lead times, changing queue positions, and data/modeling requirements.
So what
  • Prioritize accurate models and timely data submission to avoid delays and re-studies.
  • Plan project schedules and budgets to accommodate long queue timelines and potential upgrade costs.
  • Engage early with transmission owners and operators to clarify study requirements and queue milestones.
  • Anticipate contract and financial milestones (e.g., deposits, interconnection agreements) as part of project readiness.
