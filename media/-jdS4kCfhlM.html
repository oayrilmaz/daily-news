Accelerate Physical AI Evaluation and Model Updates with Nomadic on Google Cloud

Nomadic AI on Google Cloud automates conversion of raw video and sensor telemetry into curated retrain datasets to speed model updates and isolate failure root causes. It aligns visual inputs with LiDAR, IMU and control data, exports high-value failure clips into Vertex AI pipelines, and can be deployed inside a Google Cloud VPC to preserve data sovereignty.

Watch on YouTube
Key points
  • Automated failure triage: isolate edge-case and safety‑critical moments in minutes, removing manual video review.
  • Cross-stack evidence: time-align video with LiDAR, IMU and control commands to pinpoint perception or motion-plan errors.
  • Automated retrains: export curated failure clips directly into Vertex AI pipelines for continuous evaluation and model refinement.
  • Data sovereignty: native deployment inside Google Cloud VPC keeps sensitive operational footage and telemetry in your secure environment.
  • Turns dormant operational video into a proactive asset for accelerated model iteration and safer autonomous systems.
So what
  • For grid and asset-inspection teams using autonomous drones/robots, Nomadic can shorten the loop between field failure and model update, improving inspection reliability.
  • Automated cross-modal root-cause evidence reduces time and cost spent on manual review of safety‑critical events.
  • Integration with Vertex AI enables continuous model evaluation and retraining workflows that fit existing cloud ML pipelines.
  • VPC-native deployment addresses operational data governance concerns for sensitive site footage and telemetry.
  • Useful for EPCs and operators adopting autonomy or robotics to accelerate safe, iterative improvements in field AI systems.
