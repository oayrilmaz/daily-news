COSMOS Community & Safety Guidelines

Version: 2026-11-12.1
These Guidelines supplement the Terms of Service and apply to Personal, Business, Public, comments, professional connections, jobs, uploaded media, and historical/future moments.

Respect people and their privacy

Don't threaten, stalk, dox, harass, bully, or impersonate people. Never share another person's private address, sensitive identity information, intimate media, or private messages without permission. Follow the audience controls, and remember that recipients may retain copies.

Protect children

Do not upload sexual content involving minors, attempt to contact a minor for sexual exploitation, or solicit a minor's identifying or intimate material. Report suspected child exploitation to appropriate authorities. Only people age 13 or older may independently register; commercial/hiring features are for adults.

Respect authenticity and copyright

Don't fabricate professional credentials, employment offers, resumes, third-party endorsements, article citations, or evidence for news claims. Don't post copyrighted photos, music, books, videos, or software without permission or other lawful basis. Clearly distinguish verified facts, questions, opinions, fiction, predictions, and AI-generated outputs. No fake traffic, artificial engagement, spam, or bot harassment.

Business and Jobs

No fake vacancies, unlawful discriminatory hiring requirements, recruitment scams, pay-to-apply fraud, pyramid schemes, or unauthorized sharing of applicants' CVs. Do not use candidate suggestions as automated final hiring decisions. Employers must follow applicable employment laws and conduct independent verification.

Historical and future moments

A moment placed at a BCE, historical, or future date must not mislead viewers into believing it was actually created or delivered at that historical time. Do not use sealed future content to threaten or manipulate recipients or to substitute for urgent or emergency communications.

Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive content, suspected impersonation, unlawful data collection, or copyright issues to admin@ptdtoday.com with a link or description. Copyright notices may be directed to admin@ptdtoday.com. Where permitted by law, we may warn, restrict, remove material, suspend accounts, cooperate with appropriate authorities, or review an appeal. We cannot guarantee immediate review of every submission.
