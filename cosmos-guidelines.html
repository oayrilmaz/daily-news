COSMOS Community & Safety Guidelines

Version: 2026-11-13.1
These Guidelines supplement the Terms of Service and apply to Personal, Business, Public, comments, professional connections, jobs, uploaded media, and historical/future moments.

Respect people and their privacy

Don't threaten, stalk, dox, harass, bully, or impersonate people. Never share another person's private address, sensitive identity information, intimate media, or private messages without permission. Follow the audience controls, and remember that recipients may retain copies.

Protect children

Do not upload sexual content involving minors, attempt to contact a minor for sexual exploitation, or solicit a minor's identifying or intimate material. Report suspected child exploitation to appropriate authorities. Only people age 13 or older may independently register; commercial/hiring features are for adults.

Respect authenticity and copyright

Don't fabricate professional credentials, employment offers, resumes, third-party endorsements, article citations, or evidence for news claims. Upload original content, not someone else's videos or music. You may upload media you made or for which you have all necessary rights and permissions. Never repost unlicensed movies, TV and sports clips, music videos, songs, recordings, photographs, or content downloaded from social-media accounts. A music subscription or attribution is not upload permission. If you are unsure whether you have rights, do not upload the material; share a lawful link to its original source where permitted. Uploaders are responsible for their own content and may be liable for infringements under applicable law. Clearly distinguish verified facts, questions, opinions, fiction, predictions, and AI-generated outputs. No fake traffic, artificial engagement, spam, or bot harassment.

Business and Jobs

No fake vacancies, unlawful discriminatory hiring requirements, recruitment scams, pay-to-apply fraud, pyramid schemes, or unauthorized sharing of applicants' CVs. Do not use candidate suggestions as automated final hiring decisions. Employers must follow applicable employment laws and conduct independent verification.

Historical and future moments

A moment placed at a BCE, historical, or future date must not mislead viewers into believing it was actually created or delivered at that historical time. Do not use sealed future content to threaten or manipulate recipients or to substitute for urgent or emergency communications.

Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive content, suspected impersonation, unlawful data collection, or copyright issues to admin@ptdtoday.com with a link or description. Copyright owners may email copyright notices to the same address with identification of the work, disputed URL, contact information, and the legally required statements and signature. We maintain a repeat-infringer policy and may disable content or terminate accounts for repeated violations. Where permitted by law, we may warn, restrict, remove material, suspend accounts, cooperate with appropriate authorities, or review an appeal. We cannot guarantee immediate review of every submission, and we do not pre-screen every upload. Do not repost content after it has been removed.

Be a good member of COSMOS

Do not threaten other members, share their private information or contacts, circulate harmful malware, harvest data, impersonate employers, or use COSMOS for unlawful or deceptive transactions. Report suspected scams, harassment or unauthorized uploads to admin@ptdtoday.com. Safety reports are reviewed as resources and law allow; emergency situations should be reported to appropriate emergency services.
