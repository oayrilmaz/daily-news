PTD Today & COSMOS Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-11-13.1
Effective date: Upon publication
Service name: PTD Today | COSMOS (independently operated in the United States)
Privacy contact: admin@ptdtoday.com
Public contact location: United States

This Privacy Policy describes how PTD Today and COSMOS collect, use, share, protect, and retain information in the public website and the COSMOS Personal and Business services. It is a notice, not an automatic authorization to use your data for unrelated purposes. Some services or jurisdictions require separate notice, opt-in consent, or an opt-out mechanism.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and verification: email address, unique handle, display name, password-verification data (not your plaintext password), verification challenge, login activity, and preferences.
  • Personal and Business content: moments, attachments, photos, voice/video, connections, profile/background images, visibility selections, reporting and moderation information, and historical/future timeline placements, plus declarations that media uploaders have the rights required to submit media.
  • Career tools: CVs or résumés you intentionally upload, extracted professional profile, suggested matches, applications and job postings, employer-related profile details, and associated consents.
  • Interests and discovery: Personal and Business topic selections, manually selected news location, browsing/filtering preferences, and related feature interactions.
  • Technical data: IP address and request metadata processed by infrastructure/security services, device and browser signals, service logs, session tokens, cookies or local storage, and delivery diagnostics.
  • Optional camera or location: camera access, captured images or media, and user-selected or device-derived location only when you authorize a feature requesting them. A manually selected news city is not GPS tracking.
  • Public material: publicly visible questions, business postings, source links, and public moments may be indexed by search engines or shared externally.

2. Why we use information

We use information as necessary to create and verify accounts; authenticate sessions; display moments and authorized sharing; connect people; run AI analysis and career matching you request; retrieve news for selected interests/locations; operate notifications and email; detect security incidents; comply with law; handle reports; improve reliability and accessibility; and administer available paid or sponsored services. We do not use an uploaded private CV as a public job posting merely because it has been analyzed. Processing of personal data for analytics or marketing, where present, must comply with applicable law and consent/opt-out settings.

3. Visibility and sharing

Only me content is intended to be available only to the owner through authorized controls. Connections, Business Connections, and Public audiences have different permission rules. Other people you authorize to see content may copy or capture it. Public posts and links may be indexed by outside parties. If you open third-party news, social, job, or map links, their own privacy practices apply. Sealed future material is designed to hide content until reveal conditions, but absolute confidentiality or uninterrupted delivery cannot be guaranteed.

4. Service providers and third parties

Depending on which features are active, we may use Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2, AI and hosting/security), an email delivery provider such as Resend, AI processing services, notification delivery providers, analytics providers, public news feeds, and external job discovery services. Only information needed for the requested feature should be provided. We do not intentionally transmit the original uploaded résumé to an external job-discovery provider merely to fetch public vacancies; if applying requires sharing a résumé with an employer, the interface should obtain your explicit direction. Third-party tools used by the Service may change; material changes to how your data is handled will be described as required by law.

5. Security and retention

We use or seek to maintain controls such as authentication, authorization, encryption in transit where available, private media storage and rate limits appropriate to the feature. A statement of security measures is not a guarantee that every feature has passed an independent audit. No online system is completely secure. Save separate copies of irreplaceable moments. Account records are retained while needed to run the Service; deleted content may remain temporarily in backups, caches, or legal records. Acceptance history may be retained as reasonably necessary to establish the contractual agreement or comply with law. We do not promise an unverified fixed deletion timeline. Contact us for information about a particular deletion request and any retention legally required for safety, abuse prevention, dispute resolution or compliance.

6. Your choices and rights

You can select audiences for supported content, choose whether to upload media, change Personal/Business interests and news locations, manage notification settings, and delete eligible moments. You may request access, correction, portability, deletion, or information about sharing by contacting admin@ptdtoday.com. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law after appropriate verification. Residents of certain jurisdictions may have additional rights, including rights concerning profiling, targeted advertising, or use of sensitive data. If a legally required privacy-choice or appeal mechanism is applicable, it must be implemented—not merely described here.

7. Children and teens

COSMOS does not knowingly offer independent registration to children younger than 13. Users ages 13–17 should have parental or guardian permission and use limited sharing. We do not intentionally target under-13 children with public profiles, advertising, or direct-messaging features. If we learn an under-13 child has supplied personal information through an account contrary to this policy, contact admin@ptdtoday.com so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate. Children’s privacy protections and age eligibility may vary by location; we may restrict features or accounts to comply with applicable law.

8. International use and data transfers

The Service may be used from multiple countries, and information may be processed in the United States or other locations where providers operate. International users may have rights under their local laws, including GDPR/UK GDPR or other applicable frameworks, when relevant. Country-specific rights and transfer safeguards will be provided where applicable law requires them; some features or registrations may not be available in every location.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy to reflect new features, legal requirements, or providers. Material changes will be communicated as required by law, and additional permission will be requested when required. Archived versions should remain available for legal recordkeeping.
