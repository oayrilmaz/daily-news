Why did Jurassic Park emphasize the difference between gas and electric jeeps?

The short answer

Gas jeeps worked when electric ones failed during power outages.

Explore the answer

Jurassic Park highlights the difference between gas and electric jeeps to show the practical impact of power failures in the park. The electric vehicles run on rails and rely entirely on the park's electrical grid, lacking batteries and the ability to operate independently. When the park's power systems fail, these electric jeeps become inoperable, stranding characters who rely on them. In contrast, gas-powered jeeps continue to function, allowing other characters to move freely. This contrast serves as a plot device to create tension and emphasize the limitations of relying solely on advanced technology without backup systems. It also reflects the theme of the park's illusion of control through expensive, novel technology that ultimately fails.
