Is it acceptable to nest parentheses?

The short answer

Yes, nesting parentheses is acceptable but should be used carefully.

Explore the answer

Nesting parentheses is allowed, but it is important to use them with caution. Long or complex nested parentheses can confuse readers, making it difficult to track where the parenthetical sections begin and end. In formal writing such as serious papers and letters, it is best to avoid nested parentheses altogether and instead rephrase the text to eliminate the need for them. In informal contexts like emails, blog posts, or online forums, short nested parentheses (just a few words) may be more acceptable. The key is to ensure clarity so the reader does not lose track of the structure. For example, long nested parentheses can be broken into separate sentences or paragraphs to improve readability and avoid confusion.

Grok's perspective
