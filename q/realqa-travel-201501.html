In Japan, how important is it to reserve rail tickets far in advance? Do places often run out?

The short answer

Advance reservations are recommended during peak times, optional otherwise.

Explore the answer

In Japan, the importance of reserving rail tickets far in advance depends on the train type, season, and service. During major holidays or peak travel periods, such as late December, advance booking is highly recommended because many trains, especially Shinkansen, may run fully reserved and sell out. Outside these times, many local and rapid trains have unreserved seating, allowing passengers to board without prior reservations, though seats may be limited during busy periods. For special excursion or limited express trains, early reservations are more necessary. Generally, travelers can book tickets closer to their travel date or even on the same day if they have some flexibility, but booking about a month in advance ensures seat availability, group seating, and potential discounts. Last-minute bookings are possible but may require some leeway for system processing.
