What do I need to know about traveling to Dubai during Ramadan?

The short answer

Respect fasting rules, dress modestly, and follow public conduct laws.

Explore the answer

During Ramadan in Dubai, eating, drinking, and smoking in public during daylight hours are prohibited for everyone except children. Restaurants generally offer only take-away service until after sunset, and designated eating areas may be available in places like Dubai Mall. Shopping malls remain open and provide relief from the heat, but food establishments inside are closed during the day. Dress codes require modest clothing: no bare shoulders, plunging necklines, short shorts, mini skirts, offensive clothing, or see-through garments. Public displays of affection are also not allowed. Hotels may have specific policies on room service during Ramadan. Observing these cultural norms and laws ensures respectful and trouble-free travel during this period.
