Meaning of The author takes full responsibility

The short answer

It means authors remain accountable for their paper's content.

Explore the answer

The phrase "The authors take full responsibility" in academic papers, especially those involving AI, is a reaffirmation that the authors are accountable for the content they publish. Historically, mathematicians were less accustomed to disclosures about AI use, leading to various ways of stating that AI contributed but did not replace the authors' work. The statement itself is not new or unique; authors have always been responsible for issues like plagiarism, fraud, or copyright violations. Including this phrase does not change the authors' accountability but serves as a formal reminder that they stand behind their work and its integrity.
