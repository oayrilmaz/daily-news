When was the American War of Independence first called a Revolution ?

The short answer

The American War of Independence was called a Revolution from 1776.

Explore the answer

The term 'Revolution' was used to describe the American War of Independence from its early stages. As early as April 1776, figures like William Henry Drayton explicitly referred to it as a revolution, drawing parallels to the earlier English Glorious Revolution of 1688. Thomas Paine also praised the conflict as a 'revolution' in 1778, highlighting its virtuous nature. By 1779, the Continental Congress published works titled with the term 'American Revolution,' indicating official recognition. Although some contemporaries viewed the conflict as a British civil war, the term 'Revolution' became the dominant way Americans referred to the war by its conclusion, replacing earlier references such as the Glorious Revolution.
