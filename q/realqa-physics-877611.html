Are we still looking for magnetic monopoles in 2026?

The short answer

Yes, the search for magnetic monopoles continues in 2026.

Explore the answer

Magnetic monopoles have been a subject of experimental search since Dirac's 1931 prediction. Early experiments, such as the 1982 Stanford superconducting loop detector, reported a possible event but subsequent improved setups found no evidence. Modern observatories like IceCube have pushed the upper limits on monopole flux much lower, yet no confirmed detections exist. High-energy monopoles above 100 GeV have also been ruled out by detectors like the LHC ATLAS below 120 GeV. Despite these null results, the search continues because discovering magnetic monopoles would have significant implications for physics, including quantum gravity and extensions of the Standard Model. Thus, ongoing research efforts remain scientifically important and active as of 2026.
