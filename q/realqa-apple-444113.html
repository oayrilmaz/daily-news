How to prevent MacBook Pro notch from hiding menu bar icons (from the right)

The short answer

Use Bartender app to manage menu bar icons and avoid the notch.

Explore the answer

To prevent the MacBook Pro notch from hiding menu bar icons on the right side, the recommended solution is to use the Bartender app. Bartender offers a feature called Bartender Bar that moves icons to a separate space below the menu bar, ensuring they are not obscured by the notch. It also allows users to reduce the spacing between icons, fitting more icons onscreen before reaching the notch area. While Bartender is paid software with a one-time fee of $16 and a free 4-week trial, free alternatives like Dozer and HiddenBar do not prevent icons from being hidden behind the notch when expanded or adjust icon spacing. Note that Bartender has recently changed ownership, and the new owner has not clarified their monetization strategy.

Grok's perspective

The notch's effect on right-side menu bar icons can vary with icon count, resolution, and macOS settings, so outcomes from any adjustment may differ across setups without a single reliable fix.
