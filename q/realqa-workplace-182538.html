How do I tell my boss I won t do extra work without being paid more?

The short answer

Set clear boundaries and manage expectations about your workload.

Explore the answer

To communicate to your boss that you won't do extra work without additional pay, establish clear limits on your capacity. When assigned extra tasks, inform your boss that you will try but may not meet the deadline due to your current workload. If you fail to complete the work on time, it signals your true capacity and discourages further unpaid work demands. Consistently managing expectations helps your manager understand your limits. Avoid overextending yourself to meet unrealistic deadlines, as this can lead to being expected to do more for free in the future. Being honest about your workload and deadlines encourages a healthier work-life balance and reduces stress. Employers typically prefer to retain employees rather than risk turnover over small increases in workload without compensation.
