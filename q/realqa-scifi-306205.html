What s the origin of this Harry Potter book title?

The short answer

It originated as a family joke about a missing cauldron.

Explore the answer

The title 'Harry Potter and the Missing Cauldron' was created by a family as part of a creative project involving stair risers designed to look like book spines. After discovering an old, large cast iron cauldron missing from their property, the family used the idea of a 'missing cauldron' as a playful book title. This was not an official Harry Potter book title but rather a humorous and fitting choice among many suggested titles for their art project. The project connected multiple generations of the family and incorporated their love of books and creativity, with the missing cauldron story adding a personal and mysterious element to the theme.
