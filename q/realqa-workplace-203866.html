Is it good to mention you had previously interviewed with the same company?

The short answer

Yes, it is good to mention previous interviews with the company.

Explore the answer

Mentioning that you have previously interviewed with the same company can be beneficial. It allows the interviewers to refer to their earlier notes, potentially speeding up the process by avoiding repetition of information that remains valid. Additionally, if you were not hired before due to specific reasons that have since changed, they can reassess your application more efficiently. However, there is a chance that previous notes may not be available or the interviewer may have left, which means you might go through the entire process again. Despite this, mentioning your prior interview experience is unlikely to harm your chances and can provide useful context for the hiring team.
