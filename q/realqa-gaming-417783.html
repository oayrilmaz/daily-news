How can auto-bridge builders exist in vanilla Java Minecraft?

The short answer

By using sticky pistons and waterlogged blocks with short pulses.

Explore the answer

In vanilla Java Minecraft, auto-bridge builders can exist by exploiting the behavior of sticky pistons and waterlogged blocks. When a sticky piston is powered with a very short redstone pulse (one tick), it pushes a waterlogged block forward but does not pull it back, allowing the block to retain its waterlogged state. This means a single water source block can be moved. By pushing two waterlogged blocks, the water flows to create a new infinite water source. Players can then use an armor stand with frost walker boots to freeze this water into temporary ice, which remains solid in areas without skylight, such as The End, preventing it from melting. This mechanism enables the creation of auto-bridge builders in vanilla Java Minecraft without mods.

Vanilla Java Minecraft redstone systems can produce automated structures, but their behavior depends on version-specific mechanics that may shift or break unexpectedly. Players should test any such setup directly rather than assuming permanence.
