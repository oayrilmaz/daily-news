Can a white dwarf blow up by cooling down?

The short answer

No, white dwarfs do not blow up by cooling down.

Explore the answer

White dwarfs start very hot and cool over time, causing contraction and increased density. While theoretically cooling could raise density enough to trigger fusion and a supernova, typical white dwarfs formed through normal stellar evolution are not massive or dense enough to explode this way. The most massive white dwarfs are already very dense and degenerate, so cooling has little structural effect. In rare cases, more massive white dwarfs near instability might collapse into neutron stars rather than explode. Thus, cooling alone does not cause a white dwarf to blow up.
