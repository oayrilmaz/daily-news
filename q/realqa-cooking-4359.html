What are the major differences between yams and sweet potatoes?

The short answer

Yams and sweet potatoes differ in color and are often confused in the US.

Explore the answer

Yams and sweet potatoes are distinct tubers, but in the United States, the term "yam" is commonly used to refer to certain types of sweet potatoes. True yams can be white, purple, or other colors and are different from sweet potatoes. The confusion arose because the softer, orange-fleshed sweet potatoes resembled African yams familiar to some people, leading to the mislabeling. Despite the shared name, yams and sweet potatoes are not closely related botanically, and the US usage of "yam" for sweet potatoes is more of a naming convention than an accurate identification.
