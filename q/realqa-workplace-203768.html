How to deal with overly talkative colleagues in meetings

The short answer

Use agendas, pre-meeting info, and time management to control talkative colleagues.

Explore the answer

To manage overly talkative colleagues in meetings, structure the meetings carefully. Send the main information or presentation in advance via email so attendees can prepare thoughtful input rather than speaking impulsively during the meeting. Use a clear agenda with time allocations for each topic to keep discussions focused and prevent any one person from dominating. As the meeting organizer, you can politely interrupt if discussions drag on and suggest follow-ups or additional meetings for unresolved items. This approach encourages productive contributions and helps maintain meeting efficiency, avoiding spontaneous or excessive talking that can derail the meeting.
