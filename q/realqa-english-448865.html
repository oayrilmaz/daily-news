Is the etymology of salary a myth?

The short answer

The etymology of salary from salt is partly true but somewhat mythic.

Explore the answer

The word 'salary' derives from the Latin 'salarium,' which is linked to 'sal,' meaning salt. Roman soldiers were given salt as part of their compensation, highlighting salt's value. However, the idea that soldiers were literally paid in salt is not strongly supported by historical evidence. Ancient texts mention salt taxes and rewards related to salt, but not direct payment of soldiers with salt. The term evolved through Vulgar Latin and French before entering English, referring broadly to compensation. While salt was important and symbolically connected to pay, the common belief that salary means payment in salt is more of a traditional explanation than a precise historical fact.
