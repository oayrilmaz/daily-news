Can crumbled feta cheese last almost a year?

The short answer

Crumbled feta can last nearly a year if kept in brine or frozen.

Explore the answer

Feta cheese traditionally lasts a long time when stored properly in a high-salt brine, fully immersing the cheese, which can preserve it for up to a year without refrigeration. However, crumbled feta sold in stores usually lacks brine and will spoil within weeks even if refrigerated. To extend its shelf life, freezing crumbled feta is an effective method. Freezing preserves the cheese without it turning into a solid block, allowing easy portioning and use over time. Thus, while crumbled feta without brine cannot last a year in the fridge, it can last nearly a year if kept in brine or frozen properly.
