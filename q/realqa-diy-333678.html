Washing machine drain that occasionally spews water

The short answer

Check vent stack and extend drain pipe height to prevent backup.

Explore the answer

Water occasionally backing up from a washing machine drain can be caused by several plumbing issues. One common cause is a blocked vent stack, which prevents air from escaping as wastewater flows, leading to pressure that forces water back up the drain. Another factor is the height of the drain pipe; if it is too low, water can back up during the drain cycle. Extending the drain pipe higher can help prevent this, but the washing machine hose must be long enough to reach the new height. Additionally, adding a small open container (cistern) at the top of the drain pipe extension can hold minor surges of backup water until it drains away. Checking and clearing the vent stack, raising the drain pipe, and possibly elevating the washer are effective steps to reduce occasional water spewing from the drain.
