Have all of these promised No Man s Sky features been added to the game?

The short answer

No, some promised No Man's Sky features remain unadded.

Explore the answer

No Man's Sky has implemented several promised features, such as reachable stars in various star systems, the ability to land and build on asteroids, and unique starship customization with numerous permutations. Players can also recruit wingmen for combat and fly directly to nearby star systems without using the Galaxy Map, though loading screens remain. However, some features are still missing: space stations cannot be destroyed, ships lack cloaking devices, and skyboxes still exist despite stars being in-game objects. There is mention of potential future heat-resistant equipment, but no confirmed update on that. Overall, while progress has been made, not all originally promised features have been fully delivered.
