USGS 2022 list of critical minerals lists 48 elements and only two minerals; why?

The short answer

Most critical minerals are listed by element, not by mineral type.

Explore the answer

The USGS 2022 list focuses on 48 critical elements rather than minerals because most minerals are mined primarily for their constituent elements, especially metals and semi-metals. These elements are valued for their specific properties and can be extracted economically from various mineral forms, such as sulfides or oxides. Only two minerals, barite and fluorspar, are listed separately because they are mined for their direct industrial uses rather than for extracting elements. Barite is used in drilling mud for oil extraction, and fluorspar serves as a flux in smelting. Therefore, the list emphasizes elements critical for their metal properties, while specific minerals are less critical since multiple minerals can serve as sources for the same element.
