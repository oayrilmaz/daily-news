What happens if you use stovetop pop corn to make caramel corn?

The short answer

Stovetop popcorn works but may include unpopped kernels in caramel corn.

Explore the answer

Using stovetop popcorn to make caramel corn is possible and will yield edible results. However, a notable issue is that stovetop popping tends to leave more unpopped kernels compared to air poppers. When caramel coating is applied, these unpopped kernels can stick to the popcorn clusters and blend in due to the caramel's color, making them harder to spot and remove. This can be inconvenient or unpleasant when eating. Therefore, if you use stovetop popcorn, it is advisable to carefully pick out any unpopped kernels before coating with caramel to avoid this problem.
