Flight oxygen mask with eyeglasses

The short answer

You can wear a flight oxygen mask over eyeglasses without major issues.

Explore the answer

Emergency oxygen masks used in flights are designed with a facial cup that is not tightly fitted to the face, unlike other types of masks. This design allows the mask to be worn comfortably even if the person is wearing eyeglasses or has facial hair. The primary function of the mask is to supply additional oxygen rather than to create a complete seal around the airways. While glasses may rest on the nose and could interfere slightly, they often fall off when the mask is put on and can be adjusted afterward. Therefore, wearing eyeglasses does not significantly hinder the use of an emergency oxygen mask on a flight.

Individual mask fit can vary with eyeglass styles, so users might check compatibility beforehand to avoid potential seal issues during use.
