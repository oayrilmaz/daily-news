Mustard-fried burger patty

The short answer

Sear patty, spread mustard on raw side, then flip and cook.

Explore the answer

To make a mustard-fried burger patty, start by searing the patty on one side without moving it for about 2.5 minutes until it is well browned and crusty. While the first side cooks, spread about one tablespoon of basic yellow mustard on the raw side of the patty. Then flip the patty so the mustard side is down and cook for an additional minute. This technique allows the mustard to cook into the meat, adding flavor and creating a savory crust. After cooking, cheese and caramelized onions can be added to enhance the burger. This method is inspired by Kenji Alt's recreation of the double-double animal-style burger, which combines mustard, cheese, and onions for a rich, flavorful result.
