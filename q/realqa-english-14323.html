What s the difference between resolve and solve ?

The short answer

Resolve means to conclude an issue; solve means to find a successful solution.

Explore the answer

The term 'resolve' refers to dealing with a problem or issue conclusively, meaning it is finished and no longer a concern. However, resolving something does not necessarily imply that the outcome was ideal or satisfactory; it simply means one way of handling the matter was chosen and completed. On the other hand, 'solve' means to find and implement a solution to a problem, typically implying success and correctness. Solving usually involves identifying the correct or best way to address the issue. For example, an issue can be resolved even if the outcome is not favorable, whereas a question is solved when the correct answer is found and shared.
