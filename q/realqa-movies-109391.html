Why did Achilles cry over Hector s body?

The short answer

Achilles cried because he admired Hector's honor and saw him as a brother.

Explore the answer

Achilles wept over Hector's body because he recognized qualities in Hector that he deeply admired, such as bravery, honor, and having an honorable king and father to serve. Despite killing Hector, Achilles felt a profound connection to him, seeing him as a brother who shared his most important values. This emotional response was influenced by his changed perspective after Priam's visit, which made Achilles reflect on the honor and purpose Hector represented. The tears symbolize Achilles' complex feelings of respect and sorrow for Hector, as well as a sense of loss for the values they both held dear.
