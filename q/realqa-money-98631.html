How to compute worth of my equity?

The short answer

Calculate equity worth by company value and your share percentage.

Explore the answer

To compute the worth of your equity, you need to know two key things: the total number of shares outstanding and the overall value of the company. Your equity's value is roughly your ownership percentage multiplied by the company's value. For example, if you have options to buy 100,000 shares but the company has 1 billion shares outstanding, you own 0.01% of the company. The actual value depends on the company's market value or estimated future cash flows. Since private company shares aren't publicly traded, their worth is what someone is willing to pay. Additionally, vesting schedules affect how many shares you actually own over time. Without a public market or a company sale, estimating exact value is difficult and involves risk.
