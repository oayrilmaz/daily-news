What meals are available on Russian trains?

The short answer

Russian trains offer dining cars, snacks, and sometimes meals in compartments.

Explore the answer

Most long-distance Russian trains have dining cars serving decent, middle-range restaurant food, though not gourmet delicacies. Passengers can also buy basic snacks from conductors or waiters who sometimes walk through the train. At stations with longer stops, local vendors often sell food on the platform, though this practice has decreased due to increased security. Meals can be included with tickets starting from 2nd class, typically indicated by labels like 'У2', which means services including meals. On longer trips, hot food may be delivered directly to compartments. Boiling water is available in sleeping cars, but cold drinking water might be scarce, so bringing bottled water is advisable. Premium trains offer better facilities and food options, while temporary or lower-tier trains may lack dining cars or have fewer amenities.

Grok's perspective

Information from untrusted online sources on Russian train meals warrants caution, as services can vary by route, season, and operator without reliable confirmation. Direct inquiry with the railway remains advisable.
