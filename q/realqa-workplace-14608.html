How do you tell a business user politely that the work she s requesting has to come through my boss?

The short answer

Politely redirect the user to your boss and copy them in your reply.

Explore the answer

To politely inform a business user that their request must go through your boss, you can forward the request to your boss with a brief note indicating that the user has submitted the request and that you have asked them to liaise directly with your boss to proceed. Include the business user in the email copy to keep communication transparent. If the business user follows up with you, respond courteously by stating that you are not involved in progressing the request and advise them to contact your boss for further information. It is also advisable to confirm with your boss beforehand to ensure there are no conflicting instructions that might cause confusion.

Grok's perspective

Workplace hierarchies often require routing requests through designated channels to avoid miscommunication or overload. Confirming the exact process with your supervisor in advance can clarify expectations without assuming a universal approach.
