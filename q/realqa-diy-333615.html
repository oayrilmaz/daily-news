When tiling a walk in-shower, how can I ensure the linear drain doesn t protude above the tiles?

The short answer

Use a 10mm notched trowel for thinset to match tile and drain height.

Explore the answer

To prevent a linear drain from protruding above the tiles in a walk-in shower, consider the thickness of your tiles and the thinset mortar layer. If your tiles are about 9mm thick and relatively large, using a thinset trowel with large notches, such as a 10mm notched trowel, will help achieve a finished thinset thickness of approximately 6 to 8mm. This thickness allows the thinset to properly bed the tiles and align the tile surface flush with the drain, avoiding any protrusion. The size of the trowel notches directly influences the thinset thickness, so selecting the appropriate notch size is key to ensuring the drain sits level with the tile surface.
