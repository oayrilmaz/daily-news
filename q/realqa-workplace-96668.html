How do I professionally decline to disclose my current salary

The short answer

Politely say you prefer not to discuss your salary history.

Explore the answer

To professionally decline disclosing your current salary, you can simply state that you prefer not to discuss your salary history. If pressed, explain that your past salary agreements are specific to previous employers and may not be relevant to the new position. You might also mention that you consider yourself underpaid, so your current salary does not reflect your market value. While there is a risk that the employer may make an offer contingent on revealing your salary, you must then decide how important the job is to you and whether to disclose or stand firm. This approach helps maintain professionalism and keeps the focus on your qualifications rather than past compensation.
