Early payoff of mortgages: does the interest get recalculated?

The short answer

Interest is not recalculated on past payments after early payoff.

Explore the answer

When you pay off a mortgage early, the interest rate remains the same and is calculated only on the remaining principal at each point in time. The interest you paid on previous monthly payments is based on the principal balance at those times and is not recalculated retroactively. Making additional payments reduces the principal faster, so future interest amounts decrease accordingly. If you pay off the loan completely, you stop paying interest because there is no remaining principal. Therefore, no interest is lost or recalculated for past payments; the benefit of early payoff is paying less interest going forward due to the lower principal balance.

Grok's perspective

Mortgage terms and local rules can affect how interest is handled in early payoffs, so outcomes may vary by contract. Direct review of the specific agreement is advisable.
