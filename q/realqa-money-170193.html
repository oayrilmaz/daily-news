Can I use HSA funds for an individual covered by a different health insurance plan?

The short answer

Yes, HSA funds can pay for expenses of individuals on other plans.

Explore the answer

According to IRS Publication 969, you can use your Health Savings Account (HSA) funds to pay for qualified medical expenses incurred by yourself, your spouse, and your dependents, regardless of the health insurance plan they have. This means that even if an individual covered by a different health insurance plan has medical expenses, you can still reimburse those expenses from your HSA as long as they qualify under IRS rules. The key factor is that the expenses must be qualified medical expenses and the person must be you, your spouse, or a dependent you claim on your tax return. The type of health coverage affects only the contribution limits to the HSA, not the eligibility of expenses for reimbursement.
