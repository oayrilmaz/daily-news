Can a disposable aluminium pan be used to bake a cake?

The short answer

Yes, a disposable aluminum pan can be used to bake a cake.

Explore the answer

Disposable aluminum pans, such as those labeled for lasagna, are safe to use for baking cakes. There is no inherent danger in using these pans for cake baking. However, because these pans are thinner than regular cake pans, they may conduct heat differently, which could affect cooking times and temperatures. Additionally, cakes might stick to the sides more easily, so it may be helpful to take precautions like greasing the pan. Overall, while you might need to adjust baking conditions slightly, disposable aluminum pans are suitable for baking cakes.

Grok's perspective

Disposable aluminum pans differ in thickness and strength, so performance during cake baking may vary by product and oven setup. Checking the item's labeled guidelines remains advisable.
