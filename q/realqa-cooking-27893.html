Difference between conventional oven with fan assisted and convection with fan assisted?

The short answer

They are essentially the same; both use a fan to circulate heat.

Explore the answer

A conventional oven with fan assisted and a convection oven with fan assisted refer to the same concept: an oven equipped with a fan that circulates hot air to cook food more evenly and efficiently. The term 'convection oven' is often used interchangeably with 'fan-assisted oven.' Differences you might notice between two such ovens usually come from variations in fan efficiency or temperature accuracy rather than the fundamental cooking method. If recipes or cooking times differ, it may be due to the new oven running hotter or having a more effective fan, requiring adjustments in temperature settings or cooking duration. Using an oven thermometer can help verify actual temperatures and improve cooking results.
