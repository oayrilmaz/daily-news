What can be done with bitter carrots?

The short answer

Cook bitter carrots slowly with butter, salt, and a bit of sugar.

Explore the answer

To reduce bitterness and enhance sweetness in carrots, cook them slowly at low heat. A recommended method is to peel and cut the carrots into diagonal pieces about 2 cm thick, then cook them in a pan with butter, a small amount of sugar, salt, and black pepper. Cover the pan and cook for 25-35 minutes, shaking occasionally, until the carrots are tender but not overly soft. This slow cooking process helps bring out the natural sweetness and reduces bitterness. Additionally, using salt is more effective than sugar in minimizing bitterness, so add as much salt as possible without making the carrots too salty.
