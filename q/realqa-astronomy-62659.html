how much does gravity vary on Phobos?

The short answer

Gravity on Phobos varies between about 0.0035 and 0.006 m/s².

Explore the answer

The gravitational acceleration on Phobos is not uniform across its surface. Measurements from JAXA show that gravity is strongest near the poles and weaker around the equator. Specifically, the gravity ranges from approximately 0.0035 meters per second squared to about 0.006 meters per second squared. This variation is influenced by Phobos's shape and its orientation relative to Mars, with lower gravity observed along the 0° to 180° longitude axis, which aligns with Phobos's orbit. Surface features like the Stickney crater also affect local gravity variations. These findings come from detailed gravitational mapping and analysis of Phobos's dynamical environment, including its physical libration and orbital characteristics.

Grok's perspective

Estimates of gravity on irregular bodies like Phobos depend on shape models and flyby data whose completeness is uncertain. Local variations could therefore differ from published ranges once more direct observations become available.
