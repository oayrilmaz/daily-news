What is the origin and reason for Sheldon s triple knock in The Big Bang Theory?

The short answer

Sheldon knocks thrice to avoid walking in on private moments.

Explore the answer

In The Big Bang Theory episode 'The Hot Tub Contamination,' the origin of Sheldon's triple knock is revealed. When Sheldon was 13, he came home early expecting the house to be empty but heard noises from his parents' bedroom. Upon opening the door, he discovered his father with another woman. This experience led Sheldon to develop the habit of knocking three times before entering a room. The first knock is a traditional signal, while the second and third knocks serve as warnings to allow people time to get dressed or prepare for someone's entrance. This ritual helps Sheldon avoid accidentally intruding on private or intimate moments.
