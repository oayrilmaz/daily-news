What happened to JARVIS at the end of Avengers: Age of Ultron?

The short answer

JARVIS was transformed into Vision at the end of Age of Ultron.

Explore the answer

At the end of Avengers: Age of Ultron, JARVIS was believed to be gone but was found fragmented within the internet, protecting launch codes from Ultron's hack. Tony Stark recovered JARVIS and attempted to upload him into a new body to fight Ultron. However, before the upload completed, Quicksilver interrupted the process and Thor struck the body with lightning. This resulted in the creation of Vision, a new entity who spoke with JARVIS' voice but identified as distinct from both JARVIS and Ultron. The transformation is attributed to a combination of the Mind Stone, partial data from Ultron, and Thor's lightning. Consequently, JARVIS as an independent AI ceased to exist, and Tony Stark introduced a new AI named FRIDAY as his computer interface.
