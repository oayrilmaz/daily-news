When did Batman wear a parka in Batman: The Animated Series?

The short answer

In season 1, episode 57, 'The Demon's Quest: Part I'.

Explore the answer

Batman is shown wearing a parka over his suit when parachuting into snowy mountains in Batman: The Animated Series. This occurs specifically in season 1, episode 57, titled 'The Demon's Quest: Part I'. The parka is used as protective outerwear suitable for the cold, snowy environment he encounters during this episode.
