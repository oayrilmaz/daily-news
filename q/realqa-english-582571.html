English equivalent to the Portuguese expression “entendedores entender&#227;o”, “those who understand will understand”?

The short answer

The English equivalent is 'If you know, you know.'

Explore the answer

The Portuguese phrase 'entendedores entenderão,' which translates to 'those who understand will understand,' is commonly expressed in English as 'If you know, you know.' This phrase is used to indicate that the meaning or significance of something is clear only to a select group of people who have the necessary background or context. It often refers to inside jokes or information that is accessible only to those familiar with the subject. According to Urban Dictionary, 'If you know, you know' serves as a defining phrase for such situations, emphasizing exclusivity in understanding.
