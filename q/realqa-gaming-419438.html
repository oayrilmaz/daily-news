Has any game before Morrowind had more than its 17 unique equipment slots?

The short answer

Yes, Anarchy Online had more than 17 unique equipment slots before Morrowind.

Explore the answer

Before the release of Morrowind, the game Anarchy Online, launched on June 27, 2001, featured a total of 43 equipment slots. Among these, 21 are considered unique slots, with an additional 22 conditionally unique slots. Conditional slots refer to those where some items can be worn in multiple different slots, while others are specific to a single slot. The slots cover various categories including weapons, clothing, and implants, each with multiple distinct positions such as multiple HUD slots, hands, arms, fingers, and even implants on different body parts. This total exceeds Morrowind's 17 unique equipment slots, indicating that games prior to Morrowind did have more extensive equipment slot systems.

Grok's perspective

Equipment slot counts in older games can depend heavily on how categories are defined and whether sub-slots count separately. This makes direct comparisons to Morrowind's 17 potentially imprecise.
