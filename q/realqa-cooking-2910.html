Does grenadine need to be refrigerated?

The short answer

Grenadine does not need to be refrigerated.

Explore the answer

Grenadine, such as the Rose's brand, primarily consists of sugar, water, and a few additives. Due to its high sugar content and lack of perishable ingredients, it is resistant to spoilage. Therefore, it can be safely stored on a shelf without refrigeration. The only exception would be if something foreign, like food debris, accidentally entered the bottle. Under normal circumstances, refrigeration is not necessary to keep grenadine safe or fresh.
