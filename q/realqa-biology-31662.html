Are there human cells, apart from red blood cells and platelets, without a nucleus?

The short answer

Only red blood cells and platelets lack a nucleus in humans.

Explore the answer

In the human body, red blood cells (erythrocytes) and platelets (thrombocytes) are the only cells that do not contain a nucleus. These cells are unique in this aspect compared to other human cells, which typically have a nucleus. While the gut contains many cells without nuclei, these are not human cells but bacteria, which are prokaryotic organisms lacking nuclei. Therefore, apart from red blood cells and platelets, human cells generally have nuclei.
