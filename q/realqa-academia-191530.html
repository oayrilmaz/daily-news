Why do faculty often complain about teaching?

The short answer

Teaching is often seen as an obligatory burden by faculty.

Explore the answer

Faculty frequently complain about teaching because it is often viewed as a required part of their job rather than their primary focus. Many academics enter their roles mainly to conduct research, and teaching is perceived as an additional obligation that takes time away from research activities. Furthermore, at top research institutions, teaching usually receives little recognition in hiring or promotion decisions, serving mostly as a formality. This lack of reward and the perception that teaching is secondary to research can make it feel like a chore, even if some faculty enjoy teaching. Complaints about teaching are similar to how people in other professions express frustrations about parts of their jobs that are necessary but not their main interest.
