In the Project Hail Mary novel, why is the shape of the beds important to Grace?

The short answer

The beds are oval to prevent injury after waking from coma.

Explore the answer

In Project Hail Mary, the beds' oval shape is important because the ship is designed for prolonged space travel where sudden movements can occur. After waking from a medically-induced coma, a person might stagger and could easily hurt themselves on sharp corners. The rounded, oval beds help prevent such injuries by eliminating sharp edges. Grace notices the unusual shape of the bed early on, which signals to him that something is very wrong and unfamiliar about his surroundings. This design choice reflects the ship's overall focus on safety and durability in a challenging environment.
