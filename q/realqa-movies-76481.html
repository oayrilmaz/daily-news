Did Michael Corleone ever get revenge against those who killed his wife?

The short answer

Yes, Michael Corleone avenged his wife's death by killing Fabrizio.

Explore the answer

According to the source novel and a deleted scene from The Godfather Part II, Michael Corleone took revenge on Fabrizio, the man responsible for killing his wife, Appollonia. In the novel, Michael finds Fabrizio alive under a new identity and arranges for his death by a car bomb. The deleted film scene, although never officially released, depicts Michael personally shooting Fabrizio. This act of revenge aligns with the theme expressed by Don Corleone that 'revenge is a dish that tastes best when it is cold.' Therefore, Michael did get revenge against those who killed his wife, specifically Fabrizio.
