South Korea navigation app

The short answer

Naver Map and KakaoMap are popular South Korean navigation apps with English versions.

Explore the answer

In South Korea, the most commonly used navigation apps by locals are Naver Map and KakaoMap, both of which offer English versions. These apps are widely preferred for navigation within the country. Additionally, international apps like Waze and Google Maps (GMaps) are also functional and can be used for driving in South Korea. Users have noted that Android or iOS navigation apps can provide alerts when approaching speed cameras, which is useful for drivers. Therefore, for navigation in South Korea, Naver Map and KakaoMap are reliable choices, with Waze and Google Maps as alternatives.
