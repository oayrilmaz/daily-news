Implications of the statement: Note that it is very difficult to remove Rosetta 2 once it is installed

The short answer

Rosetta 2 is hard to remove due to lack of uninstall and system protections.

Explore the answer

Rosetta 2 enables Apple silicon Macs to run Intel-based apps by translating their instructions. It is difficult to remove because Apple does not provide an official uninstall method, and the files it installs are protected by System Integrity Protection (SIP), which safeguards critical system software. To remove Rosetta 2, one must disable SIP by booting into recovery mode, manually delete the files, and then re-enable SIP. These steps are unofficial and carry risks. The inability to easily remove Rosetta 2 can lead to situations where Intel-only software is mistakenly distributed and tested without realizing it requires Rosetta, since it remains installed on the test machine.
