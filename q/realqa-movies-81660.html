Movie about a frozen person who wakes up in the future

The short answer

The movie is Woody Allen's Sleeper (1973).

Explore the answer

The film Sleeper, directed by and starring Woody Allen, features a character named Miles who is cryogenically frozen and wakes up 200 years in the future. In this future world, he encounters advanced technology such as Orgasmatron booths and confessional robots. Miles, originally a clarinet player and health food store owner in New York City, becomes involved with anti-government radicals who want to overthrow an oppressive regime. Because he is not registered in this future society, he is able to assist the rebels. The movie is a comedy and explores the protagonist's experiences in this new and strange world.
