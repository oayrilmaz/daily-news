Can flight attendants legally block me in economy class, from assisting my disabled mom in business class?

The short answer

Yes, flight attendants can legally block you from assisting in another class.

Explore the answer

Flight crews have significant authority to control passenger movement and enforce seating rules on an aircraft. Passengers are legally required to follow crew instructions, even if those instructions seem unreasonable. To avoid such issues, it is advisable to contact the airline's special assistance department before travel, request necessary accommodations in writing, and only book the flight once the airline confirms the arrangements. This proactive approach helps ensure you can assist your disabled family member as needed without conflict during the flight.
