Where can I find a daily exchange rate from EUR to USD that the IRS accepts?

The short answer

Use any consistent posted rate; Federal Reserve rates are preferred.

Explore the answer

The IRS does not have an official exchange rate for converting foreign currency to U.S. dollars. Taxpayers can use any posted exchange rate as long as it is applied consistently. For single-day transactions, use the exchange rate on that specific day. For income received evenly throughout the year, the yearly average exchange rate can be used. While any posted rate is acceptable, government-backed sources like the Federal Reserve's rates are often preferred for reliability. The key is to choose a rate that fits the facts and circumstances of your situation and apply it consistently when filing your tax return.
