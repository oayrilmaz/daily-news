What s the cheapest option for getting Malaysian currency in Malaysia?

The short answer

Using ATMs in Malaysia is generally the cheapest way to get local currency.

According to the provided information, withdrawing Malaysian currency from ATMs in Malaysia is a convenient and cost-effective option. The fees are described as normal, though they can vary depending on your card's fee structure, so it's advisable to check your card's terms beforehand. Card acceptance is widespread in hotels, restaurants, grocery stores, and convenience stores like 7-11, making it easy to use cards for many purchases. However, some local street vendors and street food stalls only accept cash, so having some cash is necessary to fully enjoy local experiences. Overall, using ATMs to withdraw cash appears to be the cheapest and most practical method to obtain Malaysian currency while in the country.
