Getting crown tickets for Statue of Liberty

The short answer

Crown tickets sell out months ahead; book as early as possible.

Explore the answer

Tickets to climb the Statue of Liberty's crown are limited to about 400–500 daily and often sell out up to six months in advance during peak times. In less busy seasons, tickets might be available two to three months ahead. These tickets cannot be bought on-site and require a photo ID for entry. Pedestal tickets are easier to obtain but can still sell out weeks before. Planning and booking early increases your chances of securing crown access. Availability for other tours, like Ellis Island hard hat tours, can be found even close to the date, but crown tickets remain highly sought after and limited.
