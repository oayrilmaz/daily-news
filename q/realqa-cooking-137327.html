What exactly constitutes an Oven Safe skillet or pan?

The short answer

An oven-safe skillet can withstand high oven temperatures safely.

Explore the answer

An oven-safe skillet or pan is designed to endure the high temperatures inside an oven without damage. Typically, these pans have solid metal handles because softer handle coatings cannot tolerate prolonged heat. Oven-safe pans can often withstand temperatures of 230°C (450°F) or higher, suitable for roasting or broiling. Materials like cast iron and stainless steel are commonly oven-safe and can handle even higher temperatures. Some nonstick ceramic-coated pans can tolerate temperatures over 300°C (600°F). Additionally, some pans include removable handle covers or silicone handle protectors to prevent burns when removing the pan from the oven. Choosing a pan with a comfortable, heat-resistant handle is important for safe and practical use in the oven.

Grok's perspective

Oven safety for skillets or pans generally hinges on specific material tolerances and any temperature ratings supplied by the manufacturer, which can differ across items.
