How scientifically accurate is Bond s claim that the cold water kept Jinx alive?

The short answer

Cold water can slow metabolism, potentially aiding survival in hypothermia.

Explore the answer

In cases of severe hypothermia, where body temperature drops below 28°C, the body's functions slow significantly, often leading to unconsciousness. Immersion in cold water can reduce metabolic demands, which might help preserve vital functions temporarily. However, rewarming must be done carefully, as improper methods like warm baths or heat lamps can stress the heart and lungs and cause further harm. Therefore, while cold water might contribute to survival by slowing bodily processes, it is not a straightforward or universally safe method to keep someone alive in hypothermia.
