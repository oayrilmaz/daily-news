What prevented the Mongolian Empire from expanding into Europe?

The short answer

Death of Ögedei Khan and European resistance halted Mongol expansion.

Explore the answer

The Mongol Empire's expansion into Europe was stopped primarily due to three factors. First, the death of Ögedei Khan required Mongol leaders to return home for succession decisions, breaking their momentum and allowing Europeans time to regroup. Second, Hungary marked the end of the Eurasian steppe, which was crucial for Mongol logistics; beyond this, reinforcements for defenders could be quickly summoned, making sustained Mongol campaigns difficult. Third, Europeans adapted their defenses effectively by building stone castles, employing scorched-earth tactics, and using steppe horse-archer strategies learned from the Cumans. These combined efforts weakened the Mongols and prevented decisive victories, ultimately halting their advance into Europe.
