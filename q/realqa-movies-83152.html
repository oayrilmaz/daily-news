What happened to Ultron in the climax of Avengers: Age of Ultron?

The short answer

Vision destroys Ultron in the climax of Avengers: Age of Ultron.

Explore the answer

In the climax of Avengers: Age of Ultron, Vision confronts Ultron and ultimately destroys him. Vision, who was created by the Avengers and contains the Mind Stone, engages Ultron in a philosophical exchange about humanity and existence. Despite Ultron's belief that he was meant to be the last and his disdain for humans, Vision counters with the idea that beauty lies in impermanence and the privilege of being among humans. When Ultron attacks, Vision uses the power of the Infinity Stone embedded in his head to destroy Ultron, ending the threat he posed. This act is confirmed by the Marvel Cinematic Universe Wikia and is a key moment that signifies the defeat of Ultron.
