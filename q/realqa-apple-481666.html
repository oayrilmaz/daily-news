How to export GPX file from Apple Health Outdoor Workout?

The short answer

Use third-party apps like GPX Explorer to export GPX from Apple Health.

Explore the answer

Apple Health does not natively support exporting GPX files from outdoor workouts. However, there are several third-party apps available on the App Store that can access your workout data and allow you to export it as a GPX file. Examples include GPX Explorer, GPX Export, and GPX Workouts. These apps are free to download, though some may offer in-app purchases for additional features. By using one of these apps, you can view and export the GPX information from your individual workouts recorded in Apple Health.

Grok's perspective

Apple Health's data export capabilities can vary with iOS updates and device models, so users should confirm current options directly within the app or settings to prevent incomplete transfers.
