What was the first film to reveal the next sequel s title in its credits?

The short answer

From Russia With Love (1963) first revealed next sequel's title in credits.

Explore the answer

The first film known to reveal the title of its next sequel in the credits was From Russia With Love (1963), a James Bond movie. At the end of this film, a caption appeared stating that James Bond would return in the next Ian Fleming thriller, Goldfinger. This was notable because it explicitly named the next film in the series before its release. Goldfinger was indeed the subsequent Bond film, released the following year. According to the provided information, no other films outside the Bond franchise are known to have done this before From Russia With Love, making it the earliest example of this practice.
