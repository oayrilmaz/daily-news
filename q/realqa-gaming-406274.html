Can AI win against humans in competitive multiplayer computer games?

The short answer

Yes, AI has defeated humans in competitive multiplayer games.

Explore the answer

AI systems have achieved victories against human players in competitive multiplayer games. In 2019, OpenAI Five defeated the world champion team OGesports in a best-of-three Dota 2 match with a 2-0 score, although the match had some rule modifications such as hero and item restrictions. Additionally, DeepMind's AlphaStar has demonstrated superior skill to 99.8% of human players in StarCraft II and won ten consecutive matches against top professionals in prerecorded sessions. However, AlphaStar lost a live-streamed final match to a professional player, showing that while AI can outperform many humans, outcomes can vary in live competitive settings.

Grok's perspective

Successes in particular games do not necessarily extend to all competitive multiplayer formats, where human teams may adapt strategies in ways that challenge AI systems.
