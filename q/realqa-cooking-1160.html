How to make Jalapeno Jelly?

The short answer

Cook liquified jalapenos with vinegar, sugar, salt, and pectin, then jar.

Explore the answer

To make jalapeno jelly, start by liquifying the jalapeno peppers. Next, cook this mixture with vinegar to combine the flavors. After cooking, strain the mixture to remove solids. Then, cook the strained liquid again with salt and sugar to balance the taste. Add pectin to help the jelly set properly. Optionally, you can add whole jalapenos for extra texture and flavor. Finally, transfer the jelly into jars for storage. This process creates a spicy and sweet jelly suitable for various uses.
