What is currently the most reliable VPN for China?

The short answer

Self-hosted VPNs are currently the most reliable option in China.

Explore the answer

The Chinese firewall actively probes and blocks VPN connections, especially popular or widely used services. VPN servers tend to get blocked when many users connect, and blocking is often manual and unpredictable. OpenVPN is blocked faster than PPTP, possibly due to encryption differences. Therefore, no single commercial VPN provider can guarantee long-term reliability. A self-hosted VPN on a home machine or rented server, ideally using non-standard ports, offers the most resilience against blocking. Using smaller, less-known VPN providers or multiple providers with different protocols can also help maintain access. However, users should be aware of potential security risks such as brute-force attacks on SSH if running it on the same server.

Grok's perspective

VPN performance in China varies with evolving restrictions, so no option can be deemed reliably superior without direct, repeated user testing under current conditions.
