Meaning of the line I m walking to John O Groats for some spastics ?

The short answer

It's a satirical, incorrect reference to Ian Botham's charity walk.

Explore the answer

The line refers to a mistaken and satirical mention of Ian Botham's famous 1985 charity walk from John o'Groats to Lands End, which raised money for cancer research. The character Brent incorrectly says he is walking to John o'Groats and raising money for 'spastics,' an outdated and offensive term for people with cerebral palsy. The term 'spastic' was once used medically but became a derogatory insult and is no longer used politely. The writers of The Office use this line to satirize Brent's attempt to be edgy and rule-breaking, which instead makes him appear ridiculous and makes others uncomfortable. The line is not factually accurate and is meant to highlight Brent's social awkwardness and insensitivity.
