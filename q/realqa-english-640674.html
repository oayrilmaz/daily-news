When are low opening quotation marks acceptable in English?

The short answer

Low opening quotation marks are not acceptable in English.

Explore the answer

In English writing, whether cursive or printed, quotation marks are always placed at the top level of the characters, using single (' ') or double (" ") tick marks. Low opening quotation marks, which appear at the bottom of the line, are considered errors in English and are not used. Such marks may appear in other languages like French or German, which use different quotation styles (e.g., « and »), but these are foreign to English punctuation conventions. On British and American keyboards, there is no standard low quotation mark, and using symbols like ,, is not standard practice in English writing.
