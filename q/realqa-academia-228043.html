Another student answers questions for me at group meetings

The short answer

Practice answering confidently and calmly assert your space.

Explore the answer

When another student answers questions for you in group meetings, it can be frustrating, especially if it happens repeatedly. One effective approach is to prepare phrases to start your answers, which helps build confidence and control over the discussion. Verbalizing your thought process aloud can also establish you as the discussion leader and reduce interruptions. Politely asking for a moment to think or calmly addressing the interrupter can help maintain your visibility. In academic settings, being visible and assertive is important, so practicing these strategies can improve your presence and ensure your ideas are heard.
