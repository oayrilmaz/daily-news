How to calculate baker s percentages for indirect doughs?

The short answer

Calculate baker's percentages either including or excluding starter flour.

Explore the answer

When calculating baker's percentages for indirect doughs, such as those using a starter or preferment, there are two common approaches. One method excludes the flour contained in the starter from the total flour calculation, which is the approach used by Jeffrey Hamelman in his book 'Bread.' The other method includes the starter's flour as part of the total flour, resulting in an overall baker's percentage. Both methods are acceptable, but to avoid confusion when communicating recipes, it is recommended to clearly state which approach is being used. King Arthur Baking's resources confirm this flexibility and suggest that excluding the starter's flour is more typical, but including it provides a comprehensive percentage. Therefore, the choice depends on preference and clarity in recipe sharing.
