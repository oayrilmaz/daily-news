How do I make very, very small dough balls?

The short answer

Roll dough into thin ropes, cut, then shape into small balls by hand.

Explore the answer

To make very small dough balls, start by rolling the dough into thin ropes. Use a knife to cut these ropes into short sections roughly equal to the rope's diameter. Then, roll each piece on a flat surface with your hand or between two flat surfaces to shape them into small spheres. This method is effective for wheat flour dough and is often used to create decorative dough bits for bread. If the dough resists stretching while rolling the ropes, let it rest for about 10 minutes to relax the gluten, making it easier to work with.
