Is it possible for the libero to score points in volleyball?

The short answer

Yes, a libero can score points in volleyball.

Explore the answer

According to volleyball rules, specifically Rule 19 regarding the libero player, there is no prohibition against the libero scoring points. While the libero has restrictions on attacking—specifically, they cannot complete an attack hit if the ball is entirely above the net's top at the moment of contact—they are allowed to hit the ball from below the net's height. This means the libero can legally score points during a match. Examples exist, such as Japan's libero Arisa Satō, who has scored points in official matches, demonstrating that liberos can contribute directly to the team's score.
