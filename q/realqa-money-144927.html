Are currency terms like USD, EUR, CNY used in all languages?

The short answer

Currency codes like USD, EUR, CNY are standardized but not universal in all languages.

Explore the answer

Currency terms such as USD, EUR, and CNY come from the ISO 4217 standard, which provides internationally recognized currency codes. However, these codes are not necessarily used or known in all languages or regions. For example, in China, the ISO code CNY corresponds to the currency commonly called RMB locally. Similarly, Canada uses CAD as the ISO code, but locally it may be referred to as CDN. Different countries may have their own national standards that translate or adapt these ISO codes into local terms. For instance, China has a national standard GB/T 12406-2008 that lists Simplified Chinese names for ISO currencies, and Russia has its own standard ОКВ:2000. Therefore, while ISO currency codes are internationally standardized, their usage and recognition vary by language and locale.
