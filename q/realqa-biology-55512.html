Is glycolysis the beginning part of fermentation, or does fermentation follow glycolysis?

The short answer

Fermentation follows glycolysis; glycolysis is part of fermentation.

Explore the answer

Fermentation is a metabolic process that converts sugars into products like alcohol or lactic acid, often occurring without oxygen. Glycolysis is the initial stage where glucose is broken down into pyruvate, producing ATP and NADH. In many definitions, fermentation includes glycolysis as the first step, followed by the conversion of pyruvate into fermentation products such as ethanol or lactate. However, some sources distinguish fermentation as only the steps after glycolysis, focusing on the conversion of pyruvate. Overall, glycolysis precedes and is considered part of fermentation in the broader biochemical context, but usage can vary depending on the source or emphasis.
