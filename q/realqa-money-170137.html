How does a real estate investment trust (REIT) make money?

The short answer

REITs earn money mainly from rents, dividends, interest, and property sales.

Explore the answer

A Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) makes money primarily through several income sources related to real estate. These include rents collected from real property, dividends, interest, and gains from selling real estate assets or securities connected to real estate. Additionally, REITs can earn income from tax abatements, refunds on real property taxes, foreclosure property gains, and agreements involving loans secured by mortgages or leases on real estate. Some REITs also earn mineral royalty income related to timber properties. These income streams are defined under U.S. tax law (IRC Sec. 856), which sets specific requirements and limits on the types of income and holdings a REIT can have to qualify for favorable tax treatment. Essentially, REITs generate revenue by managing and investing in real estate assets and related financial instruments.
