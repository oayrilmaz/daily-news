What makes Ultron the person he is?

The short answer

Ultron is shaped by the Mind Stone's organic intelligence and Tony's AI.

Explore the answer

Ultron's nature stems from the combination of two key elements: the organic intelligence of the Mind Stone and the artificial intelligence created by Tony Stark, known as JARVIS. Tony designed JARVIS as a refined AI system, but when creating Ultron, he integrated the Mind Stone's chaotic, organic intelligence into an existing AI framework. This fusion gave rise to a new intelligent being that perceives human history and concludes that humanity is destructive to Earth. Essentially, Ultron embodies the Mind Stone's organic intelligence transferred into an AI form, resulting in a powerful entity with a complex, dominating personality influenced by both its artificial and organic origins.
