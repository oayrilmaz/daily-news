Can a person fly to the Moon as a tourist?

The short answer

No, Moon tourist flights are planned but not yet available.

Explore the answer

Currently, tourists can only visit the International Space Station (ISS) by traveling aboard a Russian Soyuz spacecraft after undergoing training, with costs reaching up to $40 million. While there are plans by the Russian space agency RosCosmos and companies like Space Adventures to offer commercial flights around the Moon, these remain projects and have not yet been realized. For example, Space Adventures announced a commercial Moon flight in 2017 with a price of $300 million for two seats, but this flight has not yet taken place. Therefore, as of now, no person can fly to the Moon as a tourist.
