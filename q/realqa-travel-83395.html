What are some electronic payment systems available for use by foreigners in India?

The short answer

Visa, MasterCard, and Maestro cards are widely accepted in India.

Explore the answer

Foreigners in India can use Visa, MasterCard, and Maestro cards for electronic payments, especially in supermarkets and major cities. These cards are accepted at many ATMs, particularly around train stations, allowing cash withdrawals without currency conversion fees if declined. However, many other types of electronic payments, including popular Western mobile payment systems like Samsung Pay and Google Pay, are generally not available or widely accepted. For most purchases beyond groceries, cash remains the primary payment method. This information is based on experiences from early 2019 across various Indian states including Goa, Mumbai, and New Delhi.

Acceptance of electronic payments for foreigners in India can vary by region and establishment, so confirming compatibility with local systems is advisable prior to travel.
