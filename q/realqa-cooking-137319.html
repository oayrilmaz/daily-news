Rescuing an emulsion dressing?

The short answer

Create a new emulsion with egg yolk and slowly add the broken sauce.

Explore the answer

When an oil-in-water emulsion breaks and turns into a water-in-oil emulsion, it cannot be reversed by simply mixing. To rescue the dressing, you need to start a new oil-in-water emulsion. This is done by mixing a small amount of raw or pasteurized egg yolk with some water until it is well blended. Then, while the blender is running, slowly pour the separated sauce into this new mixture. If the sauce begins to separate again, shake it before continuing to add it. This method helps to re-establish the proper emulsion and salvage the dressing.
