How do I prevent stickiness in a rice cooker?

The short answer

Add a small amount of oil to the rice before cooking.

Explore the answer

To prevent rice from sticking in a rice cooker, you can add a small amount of oil, such as corn oil, to the rice and water mixture before cooking. This method helps the rice grains separate more easily and reduces stickiness, making it easier to scoop the rice out once it's cooked. The oil coats the rice grains, which minimizes their tendency to clump together and stick to the cooker’s surface. This simple addition can improve the texture and handling of the cooked rice without altering the basic cooking process.
