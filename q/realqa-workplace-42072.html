How to represent daily work done effectively in daily call with reporting manager sitting abroad

The short answer

Use clear plans, regular updates, and structured communication.

Explore the answer

To effectively represent daily work in calls with a remote manager, start by clarifying task expectations via email to ensure alignment. Outline your approach to each task, noting dependencies on others. Create realistic plans with estimated timelines and break tasks into milestones matching call frequency. Send status updates before calls in a consistent format, highlighting progress and challenges. Avoid comparing yourself to others and focus on building trust through regular one-on-one discussions about your growth and challenges. Consistency and discipline in communication are key to improving your relationship and productivity over time.
