Microgrid adoption at data centers drives demand for modular switchgear and controls

Signals from pilot projects point to growing interest in microgrids for large data centers as a resilience measure, which may increase demand for modular medium-voltage switchgear, protection relays, and microgrid controls.

Why it matters
Shifts in buyer type (private data centers) and equipment type (modular switchgear, controls) can change procurement cycles and certification needs, affecting OEM allocation and regional availability.

Microgrids bundle generation, storage, and control systems to island critical loads. For data centers seeking higher resilience, modular switchgear and advanced controls are key long-lead items.

If adoption accelerates, OEMs of medium-voltage switchgear and control systems could see a shift in demand profile from utilities to private infrastructure operators, with different certification and integration requirements.
