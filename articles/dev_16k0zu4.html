Distributed energy resource growth may raise distribution-substation protection upgrade needs

Increasing penetration of distributed energy resources (DERs) could be changing fault-current profiles and voltage variability at distribution substations; operators may need protection and relay upgrades to maintain stability and safety.

Why it matters
DER-driven electrical dynamics connect distribution planning, protection equipment demand, and testing workloads — operators face coordination choices that affect reliability and capital timing.

DER aggregation alters local short-circuit levels and can complicate relay coordination. These technical shifts can necessitate relay setting reviews, substation controller updates, or installation of advanced protection schemes.

If DER growth is concentrated, distribution operators may prioritize targeted protection upgrades or adaptive protection schemes that interface with DER controllers, affecting procurement for relays and testing resources.
