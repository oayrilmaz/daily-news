Substation automation expansion raises cybersecurity exposure

Signals show rapid rollout of digital substation automation and remote-control systems, which may increase exposure to software supply-chain and cyber threats unless accompanied by hardened controls and updated procurement specifications.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

What is driving it
