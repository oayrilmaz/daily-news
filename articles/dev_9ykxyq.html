Rise in substation intrusion attempts prompts AI-based anomaly pilots

Signals suggest a scenario where an uptick in anomalous access attempts at substation control systems is accelerating pilot deployments of AI-based anomaly detection. Operators are testing automated detection to reduce dwell time and improve incident response.

Why Cosmos noticed this today
