AI-driven data-center growth stressing local substation capacity

Current indications show clustered data-center and AI compute load growth could be increasing peak demand on nearby distribution substations, prompting short-term mitigation such as temporary feeders or load management.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

Clustered compute load growth may create localized substation stress and motivate expedited procurement or temporary mitigation, with knock-on effects for transformer supply chains and operational reliability.
