Interconnection timing and local permitting shaping project sequencing

Signals from jurisdictional permitting and interconnection processes indicate variable review timelines that may prioritize small-scale storage and DER connections over large substation upgrades in the near term.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

Divergent permitting timelines may favor storage and DER projects in the short term, altering procurement and construction priorities for larger grid upgrades.

What is driving it

Some permitting authorities are experimenting with expedited pathways for storage and DER interconnections to meet short-term reliability needs, while larger substation or transmission projects continue through lengthier environmental and stakeholder reviews.

If expedited smaller-scale approvals proliferate, developers may shift toward near-term storage and retrofit approaches, deferring heavy equipment procurement tied to large substations until permitting windows clear.

Why it matters
