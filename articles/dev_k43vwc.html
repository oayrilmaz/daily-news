Operational AI adoption could change reserve needs and market volatility patterns

Adoption of AI-based dispatch and forecasting tools shows promise to tighten short-term balancing, potentially reducing some reserve needs while introducing new sensitivities to forecast-model errors.

Why it matters
Combining AI-enabled optimization with high renewable shares and constrained equipment lead times creates a system where operational efficiency gains may trade off against correlated failure risk and market volatility.

Operational AI can improve short-term load and renewable forecasts and enable faster asset dispatch decisions. That optimization could reduce average reserve holding requirements under normal conditions.

However, if AI models are widely used and share similar failure modes, a correlated forecasting error during an extreme event could exacerbate volatility and create market stress, especially in regions with high renewable penetration and low spinning reserves.
