AI optimization signals lower reserve needs but elevated cyber-ops risk

Adoption signals for advanced AI-driven dispatch and predictive maintenance show potential for lowering operational reserve requirements and improving asset utilization, but they also introduce new operational dependencies and cyberattack surface concerns.

Why it matters
Balancing efficiency gains against new systemic vulnerabilities is critical for reliability; operators should plan layered defenses and fallback procedures.

However, greater reliance on AI systems for critical decisions concentrates operational risk. Compromised models or data integrity attacks could produce coordinated mis-dispatch or false-sense conditions, elevating cyber-physical risk unless mitigations are implemented.
