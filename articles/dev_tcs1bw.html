Subsea cable delays increase curtailment risk for offshore wind clusters

Current indications show that subsea cable procurement and installation delays could increase curtailment risk for new offshore wind capacity, especially where onshore transmission reinforcement timelines are misaligned.

Why it matters
Curtailment reduces renewable delivered energy and financial returns; it also complicates planning for onshore grid upgrades and converter stations.

Subsea cables are critical links between offshore arrays and onshore grid nodes; delays in cable delivery or permitting can isolate generation and require curtailment or local balancing actions.

Where shore-side reinforcement or converter stations lag, operators may deploy temporary measures but face higher operational complexity and merchant exposure.
