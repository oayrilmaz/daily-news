Scenario: EPCs adopt modular substation builds to shorten schedules amid supply uncertainty

Signals from recent procurement planning suggest an uptick in interest toward modular substation units and local assembly as a way to reduce outage windows and mitigate long lead times for bespoke equipment.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

Modular substation adoption could accelerate to manage procurement and installation risk, favoring local assembly and shorter outage strategies.

What is driving it
  • EPC firms are increasingly evaluating modular designs to shorten site installation times and reduce procurement exposure. 70% confidence
  • Prefabrication and local integration can shorten field installation and commissioning periods. 72% confidence
  • Local labor enables assembly and testing of modular units, improving turnaround times. 66% confidence

EPC firms facing longer delivery times for large, customized components are evaluating modular designs that rely on standardized skids and local integration. Modularization can shorten field installation time and reduce specialized on-site engineering, but may require changes to permitting and maintenance regimes.

If this adoption grows, local assembly and vendor-service models become more attractive, creating opportunities for regional suppliers and training programs for local workforces. However, standardized modules may trade off some site-specific optimization.

Why it matters
