Scenario: Off-grid microgrids for data centers change local utility dynamics

Increasing interest in off-grid or near-grid microgrids for data centers could reduce transmission dependency but shift operational interactions toward local utilities and increase the need for grid-interactive inverter controls.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

Microgrid adoption by data centers may ease transmission demand but requires coordination with local utilities to manage protection and commercial terms.

What is driving it
  • Microgrids provide on-site resilience and reduced dependence on distant transmission for data centers. 77% confidence
  • Microgrids commonly rely on battery systems for energy shifting and islanding support. 76% confidence

This trend could relieve some transmission constraints while requiring new interconnection agreements and potentially altering local tariff structures for resilience services.
