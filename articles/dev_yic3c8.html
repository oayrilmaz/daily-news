Complex procurement requirements can improve onshore industry development and resilience outcomes, but they also lengthen tender processes and reduce the pool of eligible suppliers. In a context of constrained manufacturing capacity, this may concentrate risk on a few suppliers and increase the likelihood of schedule slippage.

Adjustments to procurement frameworks, such as staged qualification or pre-qualification pools, could mitigate some risks but require coordinated policy and contracting approaches.
