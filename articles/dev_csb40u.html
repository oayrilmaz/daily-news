Interoperability standards for inverter communications could accelerate DER integration

Signals from standards working groups suggest convergence on common inverter communication profiles, which could lower integration friction and speed commissioning of distributed energy resources (DERs).

Why Cosmos noticed this today

Converging inverter-communication standards may reduce integration friction for DERs, speeding commissioning and aggregated services if certification and security requirements are resolved.

What is driving it
  • Clear communication standards enable more seamless integration and aggregation of DERs into grid operations. 80% confidence
  • Suppliers provide inverter hardware and firmware needed for DER deployments. 76% confidence
  • Standards and certification programs regulate how suppliers implement interoperability features. 74% confidence

Agreement on standard communication profiles reduces custom integration work and shortens commissioning cycles for DERs. This can accelerate deployments and improve visibility for grid operators, enabling more predictable dispatch and aggregated services.

However, alignment on security, data models, and certification timelines will determine the rate of practical adoption.
