Transition signals toward hybrid HVDC corridors and modular GIS upgrades

Signals from multiple planning studies point to a potential near-term increase in hybrid HVDC corridor deployments and incremental gas-insulated switchgear (GIS) upgrades as operators seek higher capacity and smaller footprints.

Why it matters
Shifts in high-voltage architecture influence equipment markets, installation expertise needs, and operational approaches for long-distance power transfer.

Planning indications suggest a strategic move toward HVDC technologies on high-capacity corridors to manage long-distance bulk transfers while using modular GIS at constrained substations to save space.

This hybrid approach could increase demand for specialized converters and SF6 alternatives for GIS, as well as for skilled installation teams. Adoption speed will depend on capital availability and standardization progress.

What to monitor: converter station procurement frameworks, pilot HVDC links, and GIS technology tenders with low-GWP insulating options.
