On-site storage and backup mixes reduce outage exposure but raise emissions and fuel risks

Scenario signals suggest operators are increasingly combining battery storage and diesel‑generator backup to harden data‑center power, which may lower outage exposure but raise short‑term emissions and fuel‑supply vulnerabilities.

Why it matters
Decisions on backup mixes affect local emissions, fuel logistics, and grid support dynamics during extended outages.

Battery energy storage systems (BESS) allow rapid ride‑through and reduce diesel generator runtime, but many operators still rely on generators for prolonged outages. Combined systems improve resilience but can increase diesel logistics needs and local emissions if runtimes lengthen.

Operators may reweight investments toward fast‑acting storage for short interruptions and maintain generator capacity for extended events. Local air quality rules and fuel supply reliability should be considered in procurement and emergency plans.
