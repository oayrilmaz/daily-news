Modular substation adoption signals change procurement and site timelines

Adoption signals for prefabricated modular substations suggest faster on-site assembly but require updated approvals and testing regimes; permitting and interoperability checks may become the new gating factors.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

What is driving it
  • Factory testing is necessary to ensure modules meet specifications prior to transport and field assembly. 78% confidence
