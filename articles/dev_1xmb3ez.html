Interconnection backlogs may slow renewable dispatch in key Brazilian regions

Signals of prolonged interconnection backlogs and queue congestion in parts of Brazil could delay the ability of new renewable projects to reach full dispatch, affecting near-term generation and regional planning.

Why it matters
Delays affect generation timelines, revenue realization, and system planning for balancing resources.

In this scenario, developers face uncertain timelines and may need to consider alternative commercial arrangements.
