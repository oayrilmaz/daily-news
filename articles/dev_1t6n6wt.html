Substation automation rollouts amplify cybersecurity exposure

Accelerated deployments of substation automation and edge AI models are increasing the digital attack surface of substations, signaling a rising need for coordinated cybersecurity measures and secure procurement specifications.

Why it matters
The push for automation plus constrained equipment supply creates a trade-off: faster modernization could reduce outages but increases systemic exposure to cyber incidents and interoperability challenges.

Automation and model-driven control at substations enable operational gains but introduce firmware, communication, and supply-chain vulnerabilities.

If cybersecurity posture does not scale with automation, a compromised device or software update could have outsized operational impacts, prompting tighter procurement requirements and potential retrofit cycles.
