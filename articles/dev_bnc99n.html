HVDC bottlenecks could limit new wind capacity

Signals indicate constrained HVDC corridor availability and interconnection sequencing risks that may slow integration of additional offshore and remote wind capacity unless pipeline timing accelerates.

Why it matters
HVDC constraints can create regional bottlenecks that slow renewables integration and affect market dispatch and prices.

HVDC transmission is a key enabling technology for long-distance renewables integration; limited manufacturing slots and permitting delays can create a choke point for projects.

Smart inverter deployments and local storage can mitigate some integration issues, but systemic HVDC capacity constraints could defer projects still in late-stage planning.

Policymakers and system planners may need clearer prioritization or interim measures to avoid a growth pause in new wind additions.
