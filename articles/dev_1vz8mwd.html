Hybrid mini-hydro and solar projects face turbine procurement bottlenecks

Hybridization of small hydro with solar is a growing pathway for stable renewables in certain African grids, but turbine and gearbox procurement lead times signal a potential near-term risk to project schedules.

Why it matters
Delays affect reliable renewable generation availability and local energy access improvements.

Mini-hydro requires specialized rotating equipment with limited global suppliers. When coupled with solar, project timelines can outpace turbine delivery, creating phased commissioning or reliance on temporary generation.

Local content and workforce training programs can mitigate long-term risk, but in the near term project sponsors need robust procurement hedges and spares planning to avoid costly outages or delayed revenue streams.
