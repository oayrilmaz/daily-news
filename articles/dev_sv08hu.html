OEM consolidation signals could reduce competitive supply and lengthen lead times for transformers and switchgear

Market-concentration indicators suggest that continued OEM consolidation would reduce supplier competition for large transformers and custom switchgear, which could increase leverage on lead times and pricing in multi-site procurement rounds.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

OEM consolidation may reduce competition, making procurement more vulnerable to single-supplier capacity limits. Early allocation and contractual flexibility become more valuable.

What is driving it
  • Consolidation lowers the number of independent suppliers, reducing competitive alternatives for buyers. 74% confidence
  • Lower competition may reduce supplier incentive to expand capacity, lengthening lead times under rising demand. 70% confidence
  • Longer equipment lead times constrain EPC sequencing and can increase schedule risk. 76% confidence

Consolidation tends to reduce options for highly customized equipment runs, making production scheduling more sensitive to a smaller set of large buyers. For buyers with multiple concurrent projects, this reduces flexibility to reallocate orders and increases exposure to a single supplier's capacity constraints.

If consolidation persists, buyers may need earlier procurement commitments or creative contractual terms to secure capacity.

Why it matters
Fewer suppliers for specialized equipment ties procurement risk to supplier capacity and increases the strategic value of long-lead procurement decisions across transmission and substation programs.
