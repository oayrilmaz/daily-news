Scenario: Grid-operational AI adoption increases optimization and cyber risk

Signals point to accelerated adoption of AI tools for real-time grid optimization and forecasting, improving dispatch efficiency but increasing OT/IT integration exposure and potential attack surfaces.

Why Cosmos noticed this today

Adoption of AI in grid operations may deliver efficiency gains while increasing cybersecurity and governance requirements. The balance of benefits and risk depends on the pace of security and regulatory adaptation.

What is driving it
  • AI can enable improved forecasting and operational optimization, reducing physical asset stress in some scenarios. 78% confidence
  • Wider AI deployment increases OT/IT integration and thus the need for strengthened cybersecurity measures. 76% confidence
  • Regulatory frameworks determine acceptable practices for operational AI and cyber controls, shaping deployment pace. 70% confidence

AI-driven tools promise better load forecasting, congestion management, and asset health monitoring. However, wider OT/IT convergence increases the complexity of cyber defenses and governance needs. Observed procurement and pilot activity indicates a push toward operational AI, but regulatory frameworks for cyber-resilience and operational oversight may lag deployments.

If AI adoption outpaces security planning, operators may face elevated operational risk; conversely, integrated AI can reduce stress on physical assets through better predictive maintenance.
