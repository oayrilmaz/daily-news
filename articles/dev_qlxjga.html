Predictive maintenance adoption could reduce unplanned outages but shifts dependence to sensor and software supply chains

Adoption signals for AI-driven predictive maintenance platforms across transmission and substation fleets suggest potential reductions in unplanned outages; however, this increases reliance on sensor networks, data infrastructure, and software providers.

Why it matters
Reduced emergency replacement demand interacts with increased software and sensor dependencies — improving reliability while introducing new asset-class exposures and procurement needs.

Predictive maintenance can identify equipment degradation earlier than traditional inspections, reducing emergency replacement needs. That could lower short-term demand volatility for replacement transformers or parts.

At the same time, reliance on sensors and cloud/edge analytics concentrates risk on those supply chains: sensor availability, firmware updates, and cloud-service continuity can create new single points of operational vulnerability.
