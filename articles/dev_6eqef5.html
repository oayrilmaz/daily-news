Grid-operations AI pilots show early predictive-maintenance signal but require sensor upgrades

Pilots using AI for predictive maintenance indicate potential to reduce transformer and switchgear failures, but effectiveness depends on deployment of high-fidelity sensors and integration with OEM maintenance processes.

Why it matters
Effective predictive maintenance could lower unplanned outages and maintenance costs, but sensor and data-integration gaps are a gating factor.

Initial signals from operator pilots show pattern-detection models can flag early degradation indicators when supported by consistent, high-resolution sensor streams. Without sensor upgrades and standardized data formats, model performance degrades and false positives increase.

Successful scaling would require investment in sensors, secure telemetry, and clear OEM collaboration to ensure diagnostic outputs translate into actionable maintenance tasks rather than noisy alerts.
