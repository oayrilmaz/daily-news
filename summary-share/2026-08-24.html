  • Supply-chain pressure for critical minerals and long procurement lead times for HV equipment interact with EPC delays and modular-substation standardization: if material bottlenecks persist while modular designs and AI-enabled operations scale, deployment speed may improve in some corridors but not where raw-material shortages and port/transport constraints remain.
