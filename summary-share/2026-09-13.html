  • Policy and market shifts toward capacity adequacy signals are increasing short-duration storage and modular HVDC interest, changing procurement risk profiles for utilities and EPCs.

  • Simultaneous moves in policy favoring capacity signals and growing interest in modular HVDC and short-duration storage may accelerate procurement of non-traditional assets, shifting capital from large-scale long-lead equipment to faster-delivered systems and changing project risk allocation for EPCs and OEMs.
  • AI-driven compute demand → substation pressure → accelerated transformer orders → supplier lead-time risk → contingency procurement of mobile substations.
  • Transmission project acceleration → higher demand for high-voltage switchgear and insulators → strain on critical-minerals sourcing → price and schedule volatility for EPCs.
