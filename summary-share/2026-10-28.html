  • Operational and regulatory complexity (modularization, HVDC expansion, cyber risk, AI-enabled operations) is shifting procurement, standards, and commissioning timelines, increasing integration risk even where physical capacity exists.
  • AI-driven load growth and rising data-center interconnection needs + transformer and manufacturing capacity signals could create a bottleneck where operational demand outpaces equipment delivery, increasing project schedule risk and forcing prioritization decisions across data-center, utility, and renewable programs.
  • Critical-material and skilled-labor constraints combined with growing storage and HVDC planning mean that even with policy support, deployments could be delayed or concentrated geographically, raising grid reliability and market volatility risks if electrification and renewable curtailment pressure continue.
  • AI-driven compute growth → data-center substation upgrades → surge in high-voltage transformer orders → extended procurement lead times → project scheduling and interconnection delays.
  • Renewable buildout + intermittent generation → increased demand for energy storage → battery materials pressure → diversion of supply to storage over transmission equipment → localized grid stress and curtailment.
