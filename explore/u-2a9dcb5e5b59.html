Temporary reef shades reduced coral bleaching in Florida Keys trial

A 2024 Florida Keys experiment, reported October 2, found temporary shade structures reduced bleaching in two brain-coral species during intense heat stress. The study was a small-scale trial and researchers say shading is no substitute for addressing ocean warming.

Why it matters

The finding suggests targeted interventions might buy time for especially vulnerable corals during marine heat events. Whether such measures can scale without harming reef ecosystems remains an open question.
