Copernicus report identifies 2026 as Western Europe’s hottest observed summer

A report published October 2 by the Copernicus Climate Change Service said summer 2026 was the hottest observed in Western Europe, surpassing the summer of 2003. France had already recorded its hottest summer, according to Météo-France.

Why it matters

Exceptional seasonal heat can increase pressure on health services, water supplies, ecosystems and infrastructure. The regional finding offers a fresh measure of climate impacts, though it does not by itself attribute individual events to climate change.
