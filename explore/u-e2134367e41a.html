WHO’s updated estimates show noncommunicable diseases account for most deaths

WHO released global health estimates covering trends through 2023. The UN Geneva briefing said life expectancy had rebounded to 73.3 years, while noncommunicable diseases accounted for 74% of global deaths in 2023.

Why it matters

The estimates help show how disease burdens and health-system priorities have shifted since 2000 and the pandemic. They can inform comparisons across countries, though data collection and completeness vary.
