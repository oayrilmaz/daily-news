Crew-13 launched to the ISS on a multinational mission

Four astronauts from NASA, the Canadian Space Agency and Roscosmos launched aboard SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft on October 1 for a science expedition to the International Space Station. AP reported they arrived in less than eight hours, setting a new U.S. crewed-flight transit record to the station.

Why it matters

The mission reflects the continuing role of commercial spacecraft and international partnerships in maintaining access to the ISS. Faster transit also highlights how launch timing and orbital logistics shape crewed missions.
